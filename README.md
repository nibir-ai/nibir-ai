## 01 / A bit about me

**I’m Nibir. I study intelligence and build software.**

Currently pursuing an **M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence**, I work at the intersection of machine learning, practical systems, and developer experience. Some projects begin with a research question; others begin with an irritatingly repetitive task I can no longer ignore.

I'm drawn to the mechanics underneath the abstraction: how gradients propagate, how agents coordinate, how datasets fail, how good interfaces make complex things approachable. I like figuring out *why* something works, then turning that understanding into something other people can actually use.

> **A recurring idea:** the best technical work should feel rigorous under the hood and effortless in the hand.

## 03 / The workbench

<sub>WHAT I'M CURIOUS ABOUT, WHAT I'M INVESTIGATING, WHAT I'D LIKE TO MAKE BETTER</sub>

| Area | The questions that keep me interested |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Intelligent agents** | How do we build agents that are dependable, inspectable, and actually useful outside of demos? |
| **Retrieval & knowledge** | How can language models use external evidence without obscuring where an answer came from? |

### A few things in the notebook

**SpectraAgent-Nd** &nbsp; <sub>RESEARCH / M.TECH</sub>  
Exploring AI-assisted interpretation of spectral data for identifying neodymium-bearing material, with a focus on making analysis more interpretable and evidence-aware.

**ConsoleShell** &nbsp; <sub>PRODUCT CONCEPT / EXPLORATION</sub>  
Exploring what a genuinely controller-native PC game launcher could feel like: a console-grade interface, designed for desktop, without sacrificing keyboard access or local ownership.

<sub>These are explorations, not claims of finished releases.</sub>
